METHOD

New phones sold come from Counterpoint Research's quarterly tables. Web page views come from StatCounter, which counts visits, so it is only a stand-in for phones in use. Yearly web figures are StatCounter's, as reproduced on Demandsage. 2024 and 2025 are left out because two copies of that data differ by up to about 1.3 points. Figures run through August 2026.
