What the web is made of: images & video

Images are the heaviest thing a web page downloads, and most are still JPEG and PNG. On W3Techs' count of websites, SVG just passed JPEG, WebP keeps climbing and AVIF is still under 2%. Video appears on about 7% of pages, yet it makes up about three-quarters of all mobile data.

Every image the web loads

Share of all image requests by format · mobile · June 2024 · right column: change in share since 2022

JPEG fell from 40% of image requests in 2022 to 32%. AVIF's +386% starts from a very small base. HTTP Archive's 2025 report has no media chapter, so this June 2024 split is its newest.

The main image on each page

Share of pages by the format of their LCP image, the biggest thing visible as the page loads · July 2025 · right column: change since 2024

An image is the main element on 85% of desktop and 76% of mobile pages. JPEG's 57% is the desktop figure; the report says mobile is similar. GIF, SVG and other formats are each under 2%.

METHOD

Three ways of counting. HTTP Archive crawls about 17 million websites listed in the Chrome UX Report and counts image requests (June 2024) or each page's main image (July 2025). W3Techs counts the share of websites that use a format at all and updates daily; a site can use several formats, so its figures add up to more than 100%. Ericsson measures traffic on mobile networks.
