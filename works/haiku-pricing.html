Haiku 5.5 costs a tenth of what Haiku 4.5 did

Haiku’s output price had risen twice, from $1.25 to $4.00 to $5.00. Haiku 5.5 cut it to $0.50, while its Artificial Analysis score rose from 17 to 43.

Caching follows the same rules on both models: a cache read costs a tenth of normal input, a 5-minute write 1.25 times, and a 1-hour write 2 times. The Batch API takes 50% off input and output on both.

Haiku 5.5 counts the same text as about 30% more tokens than Haiku 4.5, so a tenth of the price works out to about 87% less spent on the same text. The 100,000-token line is also counted in Haiku 5.5’s tokens, so a prompt of about 77,000 tokens on Haiku 4.5 already reaches the higher tier. Anthropic says prompts up to 100,000 tokens made up around 90% of requests to Haiku 4.5.

Artificial Analysis measured the full bill for each task, extra tokens included. At medium effort, Haiku 5.5 doubles Haiku 4.5’s score for 82% less per task. At max effort it scores 43, but it thinks for so long, 162,000 output tokens per task against Haiku 4.5’s 18,000, that the saving shrinks to 25%.

Artificial Analysis marks the Haiku 5.5 costs as provisional: they don’t yet apply the higher price for prompts over 100,000 tokens. Checked Oct 26, 2026.

A cheap worker for bigger models

Higher is better · Anthropic’s launch benchmarks

Anthropic built Haiku 5.5 for high-volume work such as summaries, classification, database queries and browser use, and pitches it as a subagent under Opus 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5 on coding work. It says Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 remain the better choice for complex agentic coding.

That is where the low price matters most. Opus 5.5 output costs $20 per million tokens, 40 times Haiku 5.5’s $0.50, so every task a lead model hands down costs a small fraction as much. On the Artificial Analysis index, one task costs $5.98 on Opus 5.5 at max effort (score 58) and $0.05 on Haiku 5.5 at medium effort (score 34), about 120 times less. How much a whole workflow saves depends on how much of the work moves to Haiku.

Benchmarks are Anthropic’s own results from the Haiku 5.5 launch post.

Standard prices, not a launch offer

Anthropic lists these as Haiku 5.5’s regular prices, and neither the launch post nor the model page mentions an introductory rate or an end date. Anthropic says it will keep the model available until at least October 7, 2027.
